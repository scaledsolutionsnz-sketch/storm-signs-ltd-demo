About Storm Signs

Precision-Driven.
Mission-Ready.

Founded in 2022 by New Zealand Army Sergeant Aaron Hoeflich, Storm Signs is built on the values of discipline, teamwork, and excellence — forged over nearly two decades of military service.

Aaron joined the Army at 18 and rose steadily through the ranks to his current position as Sergeant. His career includes a six-month operational deployment in Afghanistan, and a role as an instructor mentoring new recruits — instilling the very principles that now guide Storm Signs every day.

Still actively serving, Aaron brings military precision, unwavering reliability, and genuine accountability to every project. Based in Rolleston, Christchurch, the team has built a reputation for delivering on time, on brief, and above expectation for clients ranging from local businesses to national organisations.

Experience. Integrity. Results. Always.

The military values that built Storm Signs — and every sign we make.
