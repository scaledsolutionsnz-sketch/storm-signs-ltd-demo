⚡ Military Heritage

Built on Two
Decades of
Service

Storm Signs was founded in 2022 by New Zealand Army Sergeant Aaron Hoeflich. The company is built on the values of discipline, teamwork, and excellence forged over nearly two decades of military service.

"At Storm Signs, our foundation is built on the discipline, dedication, and teamwork forged over nearly two decades of service in the New Zealand Army."

Aaron joined the Army at 18 and steadily rose through the ranks to his current position as Sergeant. His career includes a six-month operational deployment in Afghanistan and a role as a military instructor, mentoring new recruits and instilling the very principles that now guide Storm Signs today.

Still actively serving, Aaron brings military precision and unwavering reliability to every single project.
